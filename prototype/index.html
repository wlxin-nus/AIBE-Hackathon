How suggestions work

For each group, we intersect acceptable temperature ranges. When they overlap, we place the mean preferred temperature inside that shared range. With no overlap, the group is flagged for review.

Empty groups receive no suggestion. Concept geometry does not establish actual FCU coverage.

Concept model supplied for this demo; geometry and heights are illustrative. Positions are manually selected. Sharing expires after 20 seconds without a heartbeat. No measured energy or carbon savings are claimed.
